Station 01 · Understand

Learn It!

Adding money is easy when you take it step-by-step. Align your place values, calculate column by column from the right, and verify your result.

How to Add Money

Follow these four simple steps for every addition problem:

1. Write

Arrange the currency amounts in vertical columns clearly.

2. Align

Line up the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands place values.

3. Add

Begin from the smallest place value and regroup when needed.

4. Check

Use estimation or subtraction to verify your final total.

Worked Example
RM1,250 + RM2,375 = RM3,625

Thousands: 1,000 + 2,000 = 3,000
Hundreds: 200 + 300 = 500
Tens: 50 + 70 = 120
Ones: 0 + 5 = 5
Total: RM3,625

Let's Understand Better!

Work out each question in your notebook, then open the drawer to reveal the answer.

Example 1: Practice

Calculate RM2,430 + RM1,560.

Reveal Answer

RM2,430 + RM1,560 = RM3,990

Example 2: Challenge

Calculate RM4,125 + RM875. Notice how regrouping takes place.

Reveal Answer

RM4,125 + RM875 = RM5,000

Teacher & Parent Note:

These examples reinforce place-value alignment and regrouping techniques aligned with Year 3 mathematics curriculum objectives.
